# 🌱 AeryHub

**Connecting Local Farmers, Empowering Fresh Choices**

AeryHub is an AgriTech marketplace developed to bridge the gap between local farmers and consumers. While farmers struggle with unsold produce, low profit margins, and competition from imported goods, consumers face rising food prices and limited access to trusted local products.

Our platform creates a direct farm-to-table ecosystem where farmers can reach customers without relying heavily on middlemen, while consumers gain access to fresh, locally sourced produce with greater transparency and convenience.

By leveraging digital technology, AeryHub supports sustainable agriculture, strengthens local food security, and contributes to the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals:

🌾 **SDG 2 – Zero Hunger**
💼 **SDG 8 – Decent Work & Economic Growth**
♻️ **SDG 12 – Responsible Consumption & Production**

Together, we're building a future where technology empowers farmers, supports local communities, and makes fresh food more accessible for everyone.
