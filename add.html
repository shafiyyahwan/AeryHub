Grading basis
Products should be graded based on Malaysian Standard (MS). If no MS is available, use the origin country standard or grading standard issued by the relevant authority.
Grade levels
Premium: free from damage and defects. Grade 1 and Grade 2: fairly free from damage and defects, with different allowed tolerance levels.
1. Freshness
Look for smooth skin, firm texture, no wrinkles, no wilting and no soft flesh. Poor freshness may affect other products in the same lot.
2. Maturity
Maturity should be uniform. Use colour or ripeness index when available, such as maturity index 1 to 6.
3. Damage
Check for bruises, cuts, cracks, pest holes, cold injury, rot, mould or disease infection.
4. Defects
Check shape defects, dirt, colour issues or outer appearance problems that may reduce visual quality.
5. Size uniformity
Products in one package should have consistent size or weight, within the allowed tolerance range.
